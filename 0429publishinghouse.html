Frequently Asked Questions

Before we begin

Do I keep the copyright to my book?

Your written agreement should clearly state who owns the copyright and what publishing rights are granted. Authors generally keep their copyright unless a contract expressly transfers it.

Does the $499 package include editing?

The standard package is presented as publishing preparation and support. Developmental editing, copyediting, proofreading, illustrations, and major manuscript reconstruction should be quoted separately unless specifically included in writing.

How long does publishing take?

Timing depends on manuscript condition, formatting complexity, revisions, author approvals, and Amazon's review process. A timeline should be agreed upon after the manuscript is reviewed.

Will my book automatically become a bestseller?

No publisher can honestly guarantee sales or bestseller status. Professional publishing gives your book a credible presentation, while sales also depend on the book, audience, pricing, promotion, and reader demand.
